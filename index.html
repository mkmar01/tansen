    Settings

    Voices under “High quality” are AI voices that run on your device (free, one-time download of 90–300 MB; the first sentence takes a few seconds). Or: iPhone Settings → Accessibility → Spoken Content → Voices → download an “Enhanced” or “Premium” voice, then reopen this app.

    iOS stops browser speech when the screen locks, so this is on by default.
